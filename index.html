Everything in one place

Built for daily use, from the first coffee to the evening wrap-up.

Planning

  • Today: top 3, overdue items, work and personal tasks.
  • Plan my day by priority and deadline, with breaks.
  • Inbox, Upcoming and Calendar with drag and drop.
  • Recurring tasks on any days you choose.

Personal chatbot

  • Free conversation on local AI (Ollama).
  • Changes your plan with your OK: Yes or No first.
  • Memory of facts you tell it.
  • Four personalities, and a bubble on every page.

Focus

  • Focus timer that learns your real task lengths.
  • Break it down into clear steps.
  • Priority matrix: urgent versus important.
  • Ctrl+K to search or run any command.

Habits and goals

  • Habits with streaks and weekly targets.
  • Routines and templates started in one click.
  • Goals that fill up as linked tasks get done.
  • Weekly review with your work-life balance.

Reminders

  • In-app cards, desktop pop-ups and Windows notifications.
  • Morning briefing and an evening wrap-up.
  • Done and Snooze right from the reminder.
  • Optional prayer times in the plan and reminders.

Made for you

  • English and Arabic, right to left, Arabic task typing.
  • Dark and light, phone to desktop.
  • Install it as an app; use it on your phone with a PIN.
  • Calendar import, backup and restore.

Good to know:
Open conversation needs Ollama and a local model on the computer. Without it, all planner commands still work.
Every change the chatbot suggests is shown first and needs your OK.
Reminders need the planner running, in a tab or in the background.
Phone access works on the same Wi-Fi with a PIN. Offline use on a phone needs HTTPS hosting.
Voice input is not included, so that nothing is sent online.
Prayer times are calculated and can differ by a minute or two.
All people and data shown are fictional demo data.
